## 👨‍🔬 About Me

I am a **molecular biologist** and bioinformatics-focused research professional with 4+ years of experience across forensic DNA analysis, mammalian cell culture, quality-controlled laboratory operations, and genomic data analysis in regulated environments.
My background spans forensic genomics casework, STR profiling, RNA-Seq analysis, and documentation aligned with GMP, GLP, and ISO/IEC 17025 quality systems.

Recent projects include SARS-CoV-2 variant analysis using retrospective datasets and multi-omics investigations in neurodegenerative disorders, where I combine R, SAS, Python, and SQL to clean, normalize, and interpret large-scale biomedical data.

- 🔬 Experience in forensic DNA fingerprinting (STR genotyping, capillary electrophoresis, GeneMapper IDX) and chain-of-custody documentation.
- 🧬 Hands-on molecular biology and cell culture: PCR/qPCR, DNA/RNA extraction, protein expression, and mammalian cell-based assays.
- 💊 Quality & compliance: GMP, GLP, ISO/IEC 17025, QA/QC systems, SOPs, CAPA support, and audit-ready documentation.
- 📊 Strong interest in bioinformatics, medical informatics, and healthcare data analytics for translational research and decision support.
